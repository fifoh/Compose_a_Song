You can compose your own song on different program carriers by tapping the screen to make a melody.

How to Use
  • This interactive is intended for use on mobile phones only: make sure your phone’s sound is on (not on silent) and the volume is turned up
  • Choose one of the four program carriers
  • Place notes by touching the screen, and press play to hear your piece
  • Change instruments and scales using the buttons
